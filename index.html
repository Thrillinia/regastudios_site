The Process

A story, thoughtfully brought to life.

  1. 01

    Getting to know them

    We begin with a conversation about the person, the occasion, and the memories that matter most.

  2. 02

    Finding the story

    We curate your family's photographs, selecting the moments that best capture a life and the people who shaped it.

  3. 03

    Bringing it to life

    Through animation, editing, and music, we weave those moments into one cohesive, deeply personal film.
